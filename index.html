CABIN GUIDE

Know before you buy

Plain answers to help you plan. Figures and timelines are confirmed per project.

01 Choosing a cabin

Start with the use: office, security, storage or living. Then decide size, number of rooms and how long it will stay on site.

02 Layout & fit-out

  • Doors and windows
  • Partitions
  • Electrical provisions
  • AC provisions
  • Insulation

03 Materials

Steel-framed construction with panel cladding. Exact materials and thickness are confirmed in your quote.

04 Site preparation

Choose firm, level ground with truck and crane access. Foundation blocks or a base slab may be needed.

05 Delivery & install

Cabins are transported to site and lifted into place. Delivery coverage and timing are confirmed by Zohan.

06 Care & maintenance

Keep roofs and drains clear, check seals and repaint exposed steel when needed.
